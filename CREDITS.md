The pack was started by Squish Succubus and OpeN. Follow [@WetHotSFX](https://x.com/WetHotSFX)
to support them.

The pack's full terms are in `README - OpenNSFW Voice Pack Terms.pdf`, which ships with the
audio. In short:

- Credit the pack and the performers, by their X (Twitter) handles, and always say it is a
  voice pack: for example `[Character Moans]: @chiyo1000nights OpenNSFW VoicePack`.
- On social media, tag voice work as **VA Pack** rather than VA. If a post might be seen as
  offensive or break the platform's rules, credit only the pack and leave out the performers.
- **Don't use these clips to train AI, clone a voice, or create content that could pass as
  the performer's own work.**
